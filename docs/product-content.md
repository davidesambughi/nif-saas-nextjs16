The problem we solve
Getting a NIF requires submitting documents to Finanças (Portuguese tax authority). The process is in Portuguese only, requires physical presence or a licensed representative, and is notoriously slow and confusing for foreigners.
Our value proposition
Remote. Fast. Done. We handle everything — document collection, fiscal representation, submission to Finanças — while the customer stays home.

Who our customers are
The Prepared Planner — American/British professional, 35–50, planning a move 3–6 months ahead. Wants everything legal and documented. Willing to pay for certainty.
The Urgent Nomad — Digital nomad, 25–35, needs NIF in 48h for a visa deadline. Price-sensitive but speed-sensitive.
The Budget Conscious — Retiree on fixed income. Can wait 2–3 weeks if the price is right.
The Bundle Seeker — Golden Visa applicant, high net worth. Wants NIF + bank account + NISS + tax consulting as a package.
Key constraint (never forget this)
The Finanças portal submission is irreducibly manual — no public API exists. A licensed Portuguese fiscal representative must submit manually. All automation is built around this step, never replacing it.
